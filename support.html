Blip support

Questions, problems, or ideas about Blip: Screen Time Game? Email blipscreentimegame@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • Screen Time isn't filling in my number. Open Blip's Settings and check that "Automatic screen time" is on. Screen Time sends the number for each full day, so it appears the next morning. You can always type it in yourself.
  • I got a new iPhone. Restoring from an iPhone backup or using iPhone transfer brings your Blip along. You can also use "Copy backup code" in Settings before you switch.
  • I lost my gifts. Go to Shop and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought them with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
